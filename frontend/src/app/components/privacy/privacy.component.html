Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to JobTrackPro. This is an open-source portfolio project designed and developed by me, Hari Thatikonda.

I built this application to demonstrate enterprise-grade software architecture. I respect your privacy and am committed to protecting the personal information you share while using this demo.

2. Information I Collect

  • Account Information: I collect your name, email address, and profile picture (via Google/GitHub OAuth or manual upload) to create your user profile.
  • Job Application Data: The data you enter regarding your job hunt (Company, Role, Salary) is stored securely in my database.
  • Email Content (Optional): If you use the "Import from Email" feature, I process specific emails you forward to extract job details.

Google User Data & AI Processing

JobTrackPro's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How I use AI: When you forward an email, the text content is sent to Google Gemini AI solely for entity extraction. I do not store your emails for training purposes, nor do I share them with third parties other than the AI provider for processing.

3. How I Use Your Information

I use your data strictly to:

  • Provide the functionality of the dashboard.
  • Demonstrate the technical capabilities of this application.
  • Authenticate you securely.

I do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information.

4. Data Storage & Security

Database: Data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL instance managed by Supabase.
Images: Profile images are stored in my Cloudflare R2 bucket with strict access controls.
Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit. I do not have access to your passwords (they are hashed using BCrypt).

5. Your Rights

Since this is a demo application, you have full control. You can delete your account at any time via the database administrator, or by contacting me directly.
