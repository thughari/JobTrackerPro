Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to JobTrackerPro. This is a personal portfolio project designed and developed by me, Hari Thatikonda.

I built this application to demonstrate enterprise-grade software architecture and to provide a utility for automating the job hunt. Your privacy is paramount, and this policy outlines exactly how data is handled.

2. Information I Collect

Account Information

When you authenticate via Google or GitHub, I collect your name, email address, and profile picture to establish your user identity.

Application Metadata

Data you provide about your job applications (e.g., Company, Role, Status, Salary) is stored in a private database record linked only to your account.

Google User Data Policy

JobTrackerPro's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Gmail Access: If you enable Gmail integration, I use the gmail.readonly scope to access your messages. This access is restricted solely to identifying job application confirmations, interview invitations, and status updates.

AI Processing: Extracted email text is sent to Google Gemini AI for entity extraction. I do not use this data to train AI models, nor is it stored by the AI provider. The processing is ephemeral and used only to populate your dashboard.

Filtering: By using the gmail.settings.basic scope, the app automatically creates a "JobTrackerPro" label to ensure I only process relevant career emails, ignoring all other personal correspondence.

3. How I Use Your Information

Data is used strictly to provide the core functionality of the app:

  • Automating Data Entry
  • Visualizing Analytics
  • Secure Authentication
  • No Data Selling

4. Data Storage & Security

Database: All structured data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL instance managed by Supabase.

Images: User avatars and uploaded resources are stored in Cloudflare R2 buckets with strict private-access policies.

Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) is used for all communications. I do not store plain-text passwords; all local account passwords are salted and hashed using BCrypt.

5. Your Rights & Deletion

You own your data. You may delete individual application records at any time. To permanently delete your entire account and all associated tokens (including Gmail refresh tokens), you can use the "Delete Account" option in your Profile settings. This action is irreversible.
